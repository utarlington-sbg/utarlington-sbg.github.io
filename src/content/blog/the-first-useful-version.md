A project doesn’t need to start with a grand plan. Sometimes it starts with one thing you wish worked a little better.

Write down the smallest question you want to answer. Can this page show a result? Can this service store one piece of information? Can someone else use the thing you made?

## Make the next step small.

Choose one part you can try, then make it visible. A sketch, a short script, or a working button gives you something to talk about with another builder.

Bring that first version to the group. Explain what you expected and what actually happened. The conversation might be the most useful thing you make that day.
